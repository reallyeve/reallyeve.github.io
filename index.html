Why I Made This Little Archive?

Hello, I'm Heather.
This is my personal archive,
where I collect things I love,
things I learn, and little moments from everyday life.

I'm especially interested in astronomy,
so I study the universe and write down what I learn, one piece at a time.

I love jazz too — listening to it,
and playing the piano from time to time.

I created this archive to keep these interests,
my photographs, and the little moments of everyday life in one place,
while learning AI-assisted vibe coding and exploring
what I can create with it.

What Moments Stay With Me?

Why Did I Get Into Jazz?

Bill Evans Trio - Emily(1969)
Bill Evans(piano), Eddie Gomez(bass), Marty Morell(drums)

One of my favorite Bill Evans performances.
Gentle, melancholic, and beautifully balanced
between three musicians listening to one another.

I came to love jazz through its freedom to create something new in the moment.
Below are some of my favorite jazz pieces for late-night listening,
followed by a few thoughts on jazz and improvisation.

Midnight Jazz Playlist. Ten pieces I keep coming back to.

01
Carla Bley — Lawns
1981
A delicate piano piece filled with space and quiet emotion. Perfect for those nights when you simply want to sit and think.
▶ Listen on YouTube
02
Astrud Gilberto — Once I Loved
1965
A gentle bossa nova with Astrud Gilberto's soft, simple voice. Warm and effortless, like a quiet evening by the window.
▶ Listen on YouTube
03
Pat Metheny & Charlie Haden — Our Spanish Love Song
1997
Guitar and double bass create an intimate, deeply personal sound. It feels like looking back on a memory that never quite faded.
▶ Listen on YouTube
04
Bill Evans — Darn That Dream
1962
A beautifully delicate ballad carried by Bill Evans' thoughtful piano. It brings to mind old loves and feelings that never completely disappear.
▶ Listen on YouTube
05
Keith Jarrett & Charlie Haden — My Old Flame
2014
Piano and double bass move together with remarkable intimacy and restraint. Like quietly looking back on a memory from long ago.
▶ Listen on YouTube
06
Oscar Peterson Trio — My One and Only Love
1964
A warm and elegant take on a timeless love song. Its gentle piano trio sound brings a little light to the quiet of the night.
▶ Listen on YouTube
07
Chet Baker — You'd Be So Nice to Come Home To
1959
Chet Baker brings a tender warmth to this romantic standard. It carries the feeling of waiting for someone at the end of the night.
▶ Listen on YouTube
08
John Coltrane — Say It
1963
Coltrane's soft, expressive playing gives this ballad a quiet intensity. Rather than asking for your attention, it seems to whisper its feelings.
▶ Listen on YouTube
09
Blue Mitchell — When I Fall in Love
1960
Blue Mitchell's warm trumpet gives this familiar standard a gentle glow. A slow, relaxed reflection on what it means to fall in love.
▶ Listen on YouTube
10
Miles Davis — 'Round Midnight
1950
A timeless midnight ballad, quiet, dark, and a little mysterious. A fitting way to end the night, leaving a quiet feeling behind.
▶ Listen on YouTube
