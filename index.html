Why I made this little archive

Hello, I'm Heather.
This is my personal archive,
where I collect things I love,
things I learn, and little moments from everyday life.

I'm especially interested in astronomy,
so I study the universe and write down what I learn, one piece at a time.

I love jazz too — listening to it,
and playing the piano from time to time.

I created this archive to keep these interests,
my photographs, and the little moments of everyday life in one place,
while learning AI-assisted vibe coding and exploring
what I can create with it.
